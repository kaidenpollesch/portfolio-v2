&emsp; **New Construction Residential Home** - Concrete Duplex

For this home, I planned and designed each side dependant on how each of the families will be using their home and what their needs are. With it being two different systems, one for each side of the duplex. Once construction was ready to drywall, the `Cat6` and `RG6` wire were installed with complete networks for the families that lived in a concrete house (which came with more limitations, why I needed the cell boosters). My scope of work was running and terminating the cables, setting equipment racks, installing Ubiquiti equipment, installing digital antennas, installing cell boosters, and a security camera system.

- Plan and Design home network for given floor plan
- Run and terminate `Cat6` and `RG6` cables
- Install Ubiquiti equipment and configure networks
  - Configure VLANs for IoT devices and guest networks
  - Block devices from spying on the network and cybersecurity risks (i.e LG Smart Tvs)
- Install digital antennas and cell boosters
- Install security camera system
